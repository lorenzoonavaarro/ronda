# Ronda treasurer

The treasurer is the bookkeeper for one Ronda circle. A signed-in member asks a question, and the agent answers from that member's circles, charges, and credit score. It can draft a payout. It never sends money.

## How to use it

1. Sign in to Ronda.
2. Open a circle.
3. Use the Treasurer card. Type a question, or press one of the three buttons: who still needs to approve charges, what the score means, and whether this round is ready to pay out.
4. If you created the circle and every contribution in the round is paid, the agent names the amount and the recipient. Confirm that payout on the circle page. Until you confirm, nothing is sent.

Amounts are in USD. The score changes are +10 for an on-time payment, −15 for a miss, +20 when a circle finishes with no misses, and −40 if a pot is defaulted.

## What it does not do

The treasurer does not charge a PayPal account, pay the pot, change the turn order, or choose a circle for you. A member who did not create the circle can ask about the round, but only the creator receives the recipient's PayPal account in a draft.
